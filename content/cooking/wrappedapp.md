Import an exported WhatsApp chat and get a tap-through story with its own soundtrack, a
dashboard of who texts more, when you talk, streaks and signature words, a full reader with
search, and share-ready posters and videos.

There is no server. Chats, media and stats stay in the browser, and the optional Ask feature
runs against a local model through Ollama or your own API key.
