# How I work and learn

## How I present
<!-- keywords: slides presentations decks talks workshops public speaking presenting -->
I like minimal slides that give me a launchpad to talk. Less content on screen, more room to speak. I want just enough pointers that I don't forget what to say, and the details come from my brain. Visuals are good. Diagrams are great. Too much text is crap, and subtitles and AI-sloppy writing are crap too. I'll reserve one accent colour for one meaning, like where things break, and use it sparingly. For a short segment, two or three slides is my sweet spot.

## How I try to learn and grow
<!-- keywords: learning growth advice first principles career advice mindset motto philosophy writing tips juniors -->
A few things I keep reminding myself. The best time is now; no time is later. Learn to be quicker, but stay fixated on first principles. Use AI unabashedly. Compare up, not down. Sit with your thoughts, it beats sitting with nonsense. And if putting words down is hard, talk it out first and transcribe the good parts, or write to yourself before writing for others.
