## 3D printing and DIY
<!-- keywords: 3d printing printer diy maker raspberry pi lamp room decor hardware creality -->
I 3D print things. For a demo day I printed little ships, which got completely dwarfed by the real ships at the venue. More recently I designed a hollow pumpkin lantern with a diya cut out of the front, sized for an LED tea light. I also have a running list of DIY room projects: a mushroom lamp made from IKEA glass bowls, and a Raspberry Pi plus Spotify e-ink display that shows what's playing. My room decor rules: at least three light sources, layer things in front of and behind each other, and height variation is vvvv important.
